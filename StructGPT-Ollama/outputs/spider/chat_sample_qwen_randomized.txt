- `MAX(Age)`: Finds the maximum age among singers from France.
- `FROM singer`: Specifies that we are querying the `singer` table.
- `WHERE Country = 'France'`: Filters the results to include only singers from France.

This query will return a single row with three columns: `Average_Age`, `Minimum_Age`, and `Maximum_Age`, representing the average, minimum, and maximum ages of all singers from France.
